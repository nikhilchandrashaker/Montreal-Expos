Timeline

May 27, 1968
The Montreal club is born, still unnamed.
Apr 8, 1969
First game: Expos 11, Mets 10 at Shea Stadium, under Gene Mauch.
Apr 17, 1969
Bill Stoneman no-hits the Phillies 7–0 in the franchise’s ninth game of the season.
Oct 2, 1972
Stoneman throws a second no-hitter, 7–0 over the Mets at Jarry Park.
1977
The Expos move from Parc Jarry to Stade Olympique.
1978
Ross Grimsley wins 20 games, still the only 20-game winner in franchise history.
1979
95 wins and the first 2-million-plus attendance; Larry Parrish named Player of the Year.
1981
First postseason: the Expos win the NLDS 3–2, then lose the NLCS 3–2.
1991
Dennis Martínez throws a perfect game at Dodger Stadium (July 28).
1994
74–40, the best record in baseball, when the strike ends the season. The team flies a banner anyway.
1997
Pedro Martínez wins the franchise’s only Cy Young Award.
2003–04
Expos play a share of home games at Hiram Bithorn Stadium in San Juan, Puerto Rico.
2004
Final season in Montreal; the franchise relocates to Washington for 2005.

Facts

  • Franchise record, 1969–2004: 2,755–2,943–4 (.484). Postseason: 5–5.
  • Parc Jarry (1969–76), Stade Olympique (1977–2004), plus San Juan games in 2003–04.
  • Retired numbers: 8 Carter, 10 Dawson and Staub, 30 Raines, 42 Robinson (MLB-wide). 83 honored owner Charles Bronfman.
  • ESPN’s own look back called Pedro Martínez-for-Delino DeShields (1994) the best trade in team history.
  • Worst trade, per the same list: Ken Singleton and Mike Torrez to Baltimore for Dave McNally, Rich Coggins and Bill Kirkpatrick.
  • Hall of Famers who wore an Expos uniform include Carter, Dawson, Raines, Guerrero, Pedro Martínez, Randy Johnson, Tony Pérez and Larry Walker.
Sources: Baseball-Reference, Baseball-Reference Bullpen, ESPN.com. Scope: Montréal only, 1969–2004.
